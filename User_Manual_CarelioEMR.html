1. System Roles & Access Hierarchy

CarelioEMR strictly enforces the principle of least privilege. Permissions are partitioned into three clearly defined user tiers:

User Role Primary Responsibilities Subscription & Admin Rights
Super Administrator
admin		 Global platform infrastructure, OpenEMR module registry, system database migrations. Full system-wide administrative control across all tenant instances.
Site Administrator
siteadmin		 Clinic tenant owner, clinician account provisioning, Caribbean demographic settings, Subscription renewals and reactivation. Sole permission to renew, extend, and reactivate clinic subscriptions via Administration ➔ Carelio Subscription.
Clinicians & Staff
dr_johnson, receptionist		 Patient registrations, vitals, medical chart notes, encounter billing, appointment schedules. Read-only subscription status. Access is suspended automatically if the tenant is deactivated.
Security Rule: Regular clinicians and front-desk staff cannot alter, renew, or view sensitive subscription billing details. Only designated Site Administrators have access to subscription management controls.

3. Module 2: Subscription Management & Lifecycle

3.1 Subscription Lifecycle Architecture

The CarelioEMR subscription engine governs account standing through an automated, server-enforced 4-stage lifecycle state machine:

Figure 4: CarelioEMR 4-Stage Subscription State Machine and Transition Rules.

3.2 Stage 1: Active Subscription

When the subscription is current and has more than 2 days remaining, the dashboard displays a vibrant green ACTIVE badge. All clinic staff have full access.

Figure 5: Subscription Management Dashboard in ACTIVE State.

3.3 Stage 2: Expiring Soon Reminder (≤ 2 Days / 48 Hours)

When the subscription is within 48 hours of expiration, the module transitions to EXPIRING SOON.

Figure 6: Warning Banner and Expiration Reminder within the 2-Day Threshold.
Automated Action: The background monitoring process automatically sends an email reminder to clinic administrators prompting them to renew. Clinical workflows remain 100% active and uninterrupted.

3.4 Stage 3: Expired / Grace Period (2-Day Window)

If the primary expiration date passes without payment, CarelioEMR immediately grants a 2-Day Grace Period (EXPIRED / GRACE PERIOD).

Figure 7: Active 2-Day Grace Period Dashboard with Operational Continuity Assurance.
Zero Care Disruption: Doctors, nurses, and laboratory staff continue treating patients without any system interruptions throughout the entire 48-hour grace window.

3.5 Stage 4: Safe Deactivation & 100% Data Preservation

If the 2-day grace period elapses without renewal, the tenant enters DEACTIVATED.

Figure 8: Safe Deactivation Panel with 1-Click Site Admin Reactivation.
🔒 100% Data Preservation Guarantee:
  • ZERO Patient Records are deleted.
  • ZERO Clinical Notes or Encounters are deleted.
  • ZERO Billing Invoices or Financial data are touched.
  • ZERO Database Users are removed.
The entire clinic database remains completely intact and is instantly unlocked the moment the Site Administrator reactivates the subscription.

4. Site Administrator Operations (How-To Guide)

To manage subscriptions, navigate to: Administration ➔ Carelio Subscription in the top navigation bar.

4.1 Renewing a Subscription

  1. Under Site Administrator Controls, locate the Quick Renewal panel.
  2. Click + 1 Month to add 30 days, or + 1 Year to add 365 days.
  3. The system updates the expiration date, sets the status to ACTIVE, and logs an entry in the audit trail.

4.2 Custom Expiration Date Override

  1. In the Custom Expiration Date Override field, select your desired date (e.g., 2026-12-31).
  2. Click Extend Date.
  3. The record is updated immediately.

4.3 Reactivating a Deactivated Site

  1. Log into the subscription management panel as Site Administrator.
  2. Click the prominent green button: ⚡ Reactivate Tenant (+30 Days).
  3. Status is instantly restored to ACTIVE, and all clinician accounts are immediately unlocked.

6. Production Daily Automated Background Process

CarelioEMR includes a daily automated background process that evaluates subscriptions across all clinic tenants:

php artisan carelio:check-subscriptions

Tasks Executed by the Background Daemon:

  • Scans tenant databases for upcoming expiration dates.
  • Dispatches email reminders when subscriptions have ≤ 2 days remaining.
  • Transitions expired subscriptions into the 2-day grace period.
  • Safely deactivates accounts where the grace period has elapsed.
  • Maintains 100% database integrity with zero record deletion.

Production Scheduler Configuration (Cron):

0 1 * * * cd /var/www/carelio && php artisan carelio:check-subscriptions >> /var/log/carelio_sub.log 2>&1
